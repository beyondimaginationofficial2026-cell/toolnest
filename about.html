About ToolNest

Simple tools for everyday digital tasks

Welcome to ToolNest

ToolNest is a website that provides simple and useful online tools to help people with everyday digital tasks.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make helpful online tools easy to access and simple to use. We aim to provide a convenient experience for visitors around the world.

Our Tools

  • Word Counter – Count words and characters in your text.
  • Percentage Calculator – Calculate percentages quickly.
  • Age Calculator – Calculate your age in years, months and days.
  • Image Compressor – Reduce image file sizes.
  • PDF Merger – Combine multiple PDF files into one.

Our Commitment

We aim to improve ToolNest by adding useful tools, making the website easier to use and providing a better experience for visitors.
